why do i even have a blog

June 2026

I don't want you to think that I think I'm presumptuous enough that what I have to say is important. But I do think that sometimes it benefits others to share my thoughts and perspective. After all, one of the main ideas of math is that collaboration of different perspectives from different backgrounds leads to innovation and new ideas. So, as I continue to learn and grow as a person, the goal of this blog is to share about what I've learned. I'm not Virgil Abloh but I want to give free game.
